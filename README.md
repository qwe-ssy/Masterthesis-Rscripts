# Masterthesis-Rscripts
This repository contains code related to my master thesis "The Effect of Front-of-Pack Nutrition Label Congruency on Purchase Intentions: The Moderating Roles of Nutrition Literacy and a Nutri-Score Intervention Message", presented for the degree of Master of Science at the University of Amsterdam. This repository contains the R scripts used in the data processing, visualization and analysis presented in the thesis.

This study investigates how congruency of nutrition label combinations (nutrition claims and Nutri-Score) influence consumers’ purchase intentions, and whether this relationship is moderated by consumers’ nutrition literacy and the presence of a Nutri-Score intervention message. A 2 (label congruency: incongruent vs. congruent) × 2 (nutrition literacy: low vs. high) × 2 (Nutri-Score intervention message: absent vs. present) between-subjects online experiment was conducted among 220 Dutch consumers, using an energy bar as the product stimulus.
